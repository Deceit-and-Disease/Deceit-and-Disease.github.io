# Deconstructing Disinformation

_In early July of this year Sam confessed that God had spoken to her. His message—a lightning bolt that cleaved a tree—was God's edict for her return to me, underscoring my role as the provider for her and our son. This portent also heralded the arrival of our daughter, destined for a Messianic role. Curiously, this revelation glossed over her and our son's whereabouts during the previous year's absence._



## The Sin of Omission
She seemingly forgot why I ventured to Seattle in December 2021—it was her insistence that led me north to forge a path for our future away from Arizona. This selective amnesia was a gut-wrenching pill to swallow, not the first time she had erased our plans and pivotal dialogues from memory. Another betrayal for the ledger. As I settled into my new city, she grew increasingly elusive, repeatedly hinting at a social media hiatus. Knowing her as an unrepentant social media devotee, I saw through the charade; her disclaimer about phone inaccessibility was a preemptive absolution for her already-decided infidelity. Her responses to my queries about joining me in Washington were nothing but smoke screens, her actions transparent then as they are now, leaving no solace in the accuracy of my predictions.

> "I went  was a single date. I fancied him, but that was it. I've blocked him now."

Her narrative omitted the boyfriend who took up residence. Nor did she disclose her STD results.
[It was chlamydia.]

While Sam vanished, my support—financial and emotional—somehow found its way to them. Sam insists it was solely my mother who aided with monthly expenses during my absence, feigning ignorance of my contributions. Sure. So, while my mother indulges Sam's gossip and bad-mouthing of her eldest, as it is the only opportunity  she has to see her grandson, she also feels compelled to pay $400 monthly for the privilege. The same mother, who records three seconds of video when she means to snap a photo, and who still worries about using a credit card on the internet, is, in secret, also the benefactor who sends monthly Koala Crates, books, toys, and clothes for my son?  I guess she is also, in secret, the mysterious benefactor who encouraged Sam to sign up for and agreed to pay for a year of internet service while Sam cheated on her son?  OF COURSE Sam believes this. This is far from the first time she's embraced ridiculous ideas to rationalize reprehensible conduct.

### Example:
She failed to mention the guy living with her, who shared much more than her space. 

However, she repeatedly expressed her expectation that I shoulder her personal expenses—car insurance, groceries, fuel, a three-month backlog of bills, overdraft fees, and even her relentless smoking habit. We had struck a deal.

On a side note, a few months prior, an injury had prevented her from contributing meaningfully to household chores. The backlog is understandable.  The glaring omissions, the demanding, never-satisfied,  unappreciative attitude, the accusations of my impropriety, the heartbreak, the risk to my health by way of her STD, and her stubborn refusal to consider the possibility that these things might possibly weigh on me are not at all understandable.

Her expectations was that I would unwittingly fund not only her nicotine fix but also her clandestine liaisons via Tinder and AdultFriendFinder. She didn't bother to mention the $800 credit card charge for moissanite AKA **fake** diamond earrings—a Valentine's Day pledge from her beau.  The concert tickets meant for them, tickets she assumed I'd be eager to claim - she never mentioned what or who prompted her broke-ass to think she could afford those. It stands to reason that there is a connection between the person that would, *fingers crossed,* go to the concert with her was also Patient Zero who prompted Sam's $900 visit to LabCorp for STD testing—a test conducted a mere week after she had reintegrated me into her life.

Yes, Sam had acquiesced to my sole stipulation in assuming her credit card debt: granting me the authority to liaise with the creditors. But, this is Samantha Ames we're talking about here, and promises are her playthings, easily discarded when they clash with her narrative manipulation. Her persistent refusal to honor our agreement now made sense—she intended to shroud the full picture from my view-a $4500 abyss of credit card debt which at face value appears to have been amassed by recklessly spending in a desperate attempt to impress the other member of her Chlamydian Courtship.

Sam used to tell me that she prayed for me to join her in life, me specifically if you can believe that, and that she felt special that God had heard and granted her prayer. It was a nice sentiment and she'd occasionally repeat those words.

#### $4,500.00 says she used the same "I prayed for you" line on one or more AdultFriendFinder/Tinder/Bumble/Badoo dates over the last year.

Is it divine mandate that led Sam to imperil my health by concealing her STD results and abandoning treatment? Was it the same deity who beckoned her back into my life, the one who whispered of our daughter's birth, also instructing Sam to wield my son as leverage, granting her clemency for her reckless choices?

I face another year perhaps estranged from my son. And what of Sam's complicit circle who endorse her divisive maneuvers? They transform reasonable requests for accountability into monstrous demands. Will they, professing love for my son, persist in imposing conditions on our bond, brandishing unmerited influence as though it were their birthright?
