# Sins of Omission  
  
_In early July this year, Sam confessed that God had spoken to her. According to her, a lightning bolt splitting a tree was a divine message for her to return to me, emphasizing my role as a provider for her and our son. This omen also heralded the birth of our daughter, a child apparently destined for a Messianic role. Interestingly, this revelation conveniently omitted Sam's and our son's whereabouts during their year-long absence._

## Lust  
She seemed to forget the reason behind my move to Seattle in December 2021. It was her insistence that sent me north to pave the way for our new life away from Arizona. Swallowing this selective amnesia was excruciating; it wasn't the first time she'd erased our plans and crucial conversations from her memory. Yet another betrayal to add to the growing list. As I settled into my new city, she became increasingly elusive, frequently alluding to a social media break. Given her unrelenting addiction to social media, her claim of reduced phone availability was clearly a cover for her premeditated infidelity. Her evasive answers to my questions about joining me in Washington were nothing but smokescreens, her actions as transparent then as they are now, leaving me with no comfort in being 100% right.  
  
> _"I went on **one** date. I liked him. No, nothing happened. I blocked him..."_  
  
Her account conveniently left out her boyfriends. Nor did she mention her STD results. Spoiler: **It was chlamydia**.

As Sam disappeared, my support—both financial and emotional—still somehow reached them. Sam claimed it was only my mother who helped with monthly expenses during my absence, blatantly disregarding my contributions. Right. So, my technophobe mother, who records three seconds of video when she means to snap a photo, and who still worries about using a credit card on the internet, was supposedly doling out hundreds of dollars monthly, not to mention sending Koala Crates, books, toys, and clothes for my son, and footing the bill for a year of internet service that Sam used to cheat? Of course, Sam believed this—she's no stranger to latching onto absurd notions to justify her awful behavior, and using my mother as an intermediary allowed Sam to pretend that I'd caused the rift between us.  
  
## Greed  
  
She failed to mention the men she'd regularly had with her, sharing more than just her space. Nevertheless, she repeatedly expected me to cover her personal expenses—rent, utilities, groceries, car insurance, fuel, _months_ of overdue bills, _weekly_ overdraft fees, and her unyielding smoking habit.  
  
She expected me to unknowingly fund her secret rendezvous' on Tinder, AdultFriendFinder, Facebook, and more. It's logical to assume that the person expected at the concert with her was also the instigator of Sam's $909 Labcorp bill for STD testing, a test done the same week she moved me back into her life.  Since she was in a cast, I was also expected to perform 100% of the household chores, which had been ignored for months, and to do them according to Sam's preferences.

But this is Samantha Ames we're talking about, where promises are mere playthings, discarded when they clash with her self-serving narrative. Her stubborn refusal to grant me basic billing permissions now made perfect sense—she aimed to hide the entire picture from me—a staggering $4500 credit card debt, apparently accrued in a desperate bid to impress her partner in their Chlamydian romance.  
  
Sam used to tell me that she had prayed for me.  That she'd asked God to deliver me into her in life, and that He'd answered her prayers. A sweet sentiment, she'd say, repeatedly. $4,500.00 says she's recycled that "_I prayed for you_" line on more than one AdultFriendFinder/Tinder/Bumble/Badoo date over the past year.  
  
> "_**I prayed for you. I asked God to bring you into my life.**_"  
  
Was it divine will that led Sam to jeopardize my health by hiding her STD results and neglecting treatment? And what of Sam's supportive entourage, the chorus of co-conspirators who endorse, embolden, and enable her destructive, anti-family actions? They transform reasonable requests for accountability into Dragons and Roaring Lions. Excuse my cynicism, but this mob has always been eager to demonize every emotion and opinion I express. Is there anyone among them pushing for reasoned dialogue, for thoughtful resolution? And will those who profess love for my son, although not kin to him,  continue to impose their terms on my relationship with him, lording power as if it were their divine birthright? Will another year pass before I see my son again? Two? Five?
