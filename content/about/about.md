_This Body of Work is Dedicated to_

# Sam Ames

## _**You embarrass me.  You embarrass yourself.**_

_**Embark on a journey, both harrowing and enlightening. This space is my sanctum, where truth spoken is sacred, where I expose scars left by a partner whose relentless campaign of invalidation warped my sense of self and nearly consumed me. It's a haven for piecing back together a shattered self-image, for separating fact from folklore, for dissecting layers of half-truths, and for discerning genuine promises from misdirection, omissions, and hollow words. Here, I reclaim my voice, seemingly muted, no matter the volume, drowned out by a cacophony of blame and deception. This is where I stand tall, unshackled from the chains of inconsistencies, broken vows, and conveniently forgotten plans.**_

To the lost, the bewildered, and the heartbroken – this blog stands as a lighthouse. As you navigate through the stormy seas of my experiences, consider this a map through treacherous waters. Learn from my missteps to spot the undercurrents of emotional manipulation. I invite you to awaken to the sly tactics of narratives steeped in perpetual victimhood, to see how emotional vampires twist our deep-seated desires for connection into weapons for their own survival. Realize that against such spirits, no act of kindness, transparency, or empathy is ever enough.

Take heart from my journey. Recognize your own unyielding value, a treasure far beyond the reach of any emotional plunderer. Dare to confront the enticing illusion of self-deception. Shine a light through the mental fog, and emerge stronger, whole.

This is more than my story – it's a testament to resilience, a chronicle spanning four intense years. Welcome to "Deceit and Disease," where each chapter unfurls tales from love’s dimmest corners, imparting lessons forged in the fire of nightmarish realities. Join me, and together let's turn the page toward healing and understanding.
