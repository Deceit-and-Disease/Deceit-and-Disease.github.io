About

From these quiet margins, I narrate a deeply personal story—my love-turned-entanglement with an ex-partner, the mother of my beloved two-year-old boy. “Deceit and Disease” is not an exercise in rhetoric. It is my confessional of intimate betrayals, a chronicle of emotional warfare, and a father’s struggle to reconnect with his son.

Ahead lies a difficult journey. This is my sanctum, a refuge where I lay bare the wounds inflicted by a partner whose campaign of invalidation, and eventual marginalization eradicated my sense of self-worth. This is a space for healing, for deconstructing the disinformation, for discerning between that which was offered as truth, and that which was withheld. Here, I rediscover my voice which, for years, was lost amidst the cacophony of blame-shifting and gas-lighting. Here I may speak freely, unencumbered by the weight of double-standards, false promises, and half-truths.

e as a beacon for the lost, the confused, and the heartbroken. As you traverse through my recounted experiences, consider this a guidebook peppered with cautionary tales. From my mistakes you may learn to discern the subtle, yet devastating weapons of emotional manipulation. I extend, to you, an invitation to you to recognize the insidious nature of a narrative steeped in perpetual victimhood. That you may understand how manipulators twist our innate longing for bonding and unity, and extract from it the placating words on which they are completely reliant. Know that no amount of effort, honesty, or compassion will be sufficuent for the manipulator, a hungry ghost entrenched in destructive cycles,

Take heed of my lived experiences. Embrace the unshakable truth of your inherent worth. For there is far greater value to you than what the narcissist extracts. Be not afraid to challenge their seductive lure of self-deception. Illuminate the darkened corridors of the mind, and rise with a full heart.

This is my testament to survival, a narrative unfolding over four harrowing years. Welcome to “Deceit and Disease"—stories from love’s shadowy precipice, lessons from the edge of nightmarish reality.
